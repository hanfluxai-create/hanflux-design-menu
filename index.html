Director Mode

Your AI doesn’t just answer.
It shows you.

  1. 01

    Presence

    Only the face, center stage. Quiet status at the edges: what is running and what needs you.

  2. 02

    Cut

    Mention something and its screen opens full size. The face steps to the corner to narrate.

  3. 03

    Point

    A pointer travels to the exact item. Everything else dims, and the caption names it.

  4. 04

    Return

    When the topic ends, the screen folds away and the face comes back. No menus to hunt through.
