questions

Straight answers.

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Is the price on a design the full price?

For the design you pick, yes: it is rebuilt with your content and comes with everything in "included in every plan". For example, an Essential site is ₹40,000$600 during the launch. Add-ons are extra and you choose them. You get one fixed price in writing before any work starts.

Why is it so much cheaper than an agency?

Agencies usually quote ₹1 to 5 lakh$3,000 to $10,000+ for a custom-designed site, because every site is built by hand and you pay for the hours. We built a factory: AI agents do the repetitive work, such as layouts, code, image prep, first drafts and testing, and our team does the parts that need taste and judgement. Fewer hours, same checks, lower price.

Will my site look like the demo?

Same design and the same quality, with your name, colours, photos and words. Want something different? We can mix two designs, match your current brand, or make a fully custom design.

How long does it take?

48 hours from payment. We call you within 24 hours to collect everything we need (your domain, logo, photos and text, and any technical setup), and your site is live on your own domain within 24 hours of that call. Before launch you can ask for as many changes as you want.

What happens after launch?

For 30 days we make small fixes and tweaks for free, like adding a button or changing a link. Bigger changes or a redesign are quoted separately, and our Care plan covers ongoing edits.

I am not technical. What do I need to do?

Send your logo (or ask us to make one), a few photos and a few lines about your business on WhatsApp. We do the rest, including setting up your web address.

Do I need a domain and hosting?

You need a web address (domain) in your own name, usually under ₹1,500under $20 a year. We help you buy it and set everything up. Hosting, backups, updates and small edits each month are covered by our Care plan at ₹1,500$40 a month.

Can you build a backend, custom features or AI?

Yes, as a custom order: online ordering and booking backends, dashboards and client portals, AI agents and AI features, and connections to the software you already use. We scope it on a call, give you a fixed price and agree a timeline that fits the work. The 48-hour promise covers websites from the menu.

How do I order and pay?

Pick a design, tap "Order this design", add any features, answer a few questions about your business and pay securely through Razorpay (UPI, cards or net banking). We call you within 24 hours and start right away.

What happens after I ask for the free plan?

We reply on WhatsApp within 24 hours with your free plan: the design that fits you best, the pages you need and a fixed price. No payment to ask, no obligation.
