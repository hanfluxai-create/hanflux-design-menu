questions

Straight answers.

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Is it really one price for every design?

Yes. Any design in the menu, minimal or full 3D, is ₹40,000₹60,000₹80,000$555$830$1,110 right now, hosting included. A custom design at the same level costs the same. Add-ons are extra and you choose them. You get one fixed price in writing before any work starts.

Why does the price go up?

These are dated launch prices. ₹40,000$555 until 3 Dec, then ₹60,000$830 until 31 Dec, then the regular ₹80,000$1,110 from 1 January 2027. The price that applies is the one on the day you pay.

What does hosting include?

Fast global hosting with HTTPS, up to 100 GB of traffic and 1 million requests a month: about 25,000+ visits, more than most small businesses get. If your site grows past that, we move it to a bigger plan and pass the cost on at cost. Your domain name is separate, usually under ₹1,500under $20 a year, and we help you buy it in your own name.

Why is it so much cheaper than an agency?

Agencies usually quote ₹1 to 5 lakh$3,000 to $10,000+ for a custom-designed site, because every site is built by hand and you pay for the hours. We built a design factory: AI agents do the repetitive work, such as layouts, code, image prep, first drafts and testing, and our designers do the parts that need taste and judgement. Fewer hours, same checks, lower price.

Will my site look like the demo?

Same design and the same quality, with your name, colours, photos and words. Want something different? We can mix two designs, match your current brand, or make a fully custom design at the same price.

How long does it take?

48 hours from payment. We call you within 24 hours to collect everything we need (your domain, logo, photos and text), and your site is live on your own domain within 24 hours of that call. Before launch you can ask for as many changes as you want.

Why do add-ons have a monthly fee?

A chatbot, a dashboard, order alerts or a store keep running after launch: servers, databases, AI usage, email and WhatsApp sending, and our team keeping them working. The setup fee pays for building it; the monthly fee keeps it running. Costs that grow with use, like call minutes or WhatsApp message fees, are billed at cost.

Do you make apps, emails and other design work?

Yes. We are a design partner, not only a website shop: iPhone and Android apps, web apps and client portals, admin dashboards, branded HTML emails and newsletters, logos and brand kits, AI chat and voice. Apps and custom software are quoted after a short call, with their own timeline.

What happens after launch?

For 30 days we make small fixes and tweaks for free, like adding a button or changing a link. After that, the Care plan covers monthly edits, or we quote bigger changes separately.

How do I order and pay?

Pick a design, tap "Order this design", add any features, answer a few questions about your business and pay securely through Razorpay (UPI, cards or net banking). We call you within 24 hours and start right away.
