03How it goes

Six weeks, four steps.

A typical brand project. You see real work every Friday and you always know what happens next.

  1. 1

    Week 1

    Brief

    One long call and a short questionnaire. I come back with a written brief and three directions in words, before anything is drawn.

  2. 2

    Weeks 2 to 3

    Shape

    Rough clay. Two identity routes modelled far enough to judge, shown on your real product and not on mock-up templates.

  3. 3

    Weeks 4 to 5

    Refine

    We pick one route and I build the whole system: screens, illustrations and motion, with two rounds of changes included.

  4. 4

    Week 6

    Hand over

    Final files, a brand guide, source models and a recorded walkthrough. I stay on call for 30 days after launch.

05About

Hello, I am Noa.

I trained as a ceramicist, spent eight years in product teams, and now do both at once: I design brands and interfaces, then sculpt them in 3D until they look like you could pick them up.

The studio is just me, on purpose. You talk to the person doing the work, I take on two projects at a time, and nothing is passed to a junior. I work from Lisbon with clients from Stockholm to Singapore, mostly on calls that suit both our mornings.
