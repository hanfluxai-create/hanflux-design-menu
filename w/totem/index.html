How a project goes

  1. 1

    A call

    Thirty minutes. You talk, I ask questions. If I am not the right person I will name someone who is.

  2. 2

    A plan

    Two pages within two days: what I will make, when you will see it and what it costs.

  3. 3

    The work

    You see progress every other day in a shared file. No big reveals, no surprises.

  4. 4

    The handover

    Files, guidelines and a recorded walkthrough, then thirty days of questions answered.
