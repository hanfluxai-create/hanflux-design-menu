p. 12 Brew guide

Pour-over at home, in three minutes.

15 g coffee250 g water93°Cmedium-fine grind3:00 total

0:00Rinse and warm

  1. prep

    Rinse and warm

    Rinse the paper filter with hot water, warm the cup, and pour the rinse water away.

  2. 0:00

    Bloom

    Add 15 g of coffee, pour 45 g of water, swirl gently and wait 45 seconds while it bubbles.

  3. 0:45

    First pour

    Pour slowly in small circles, avoiding the paper, until the scale reads 150 g.

  4. 1:15

    Second pour

    Top up to 250 g with a steady stream and keep the bed level.

  5. 2:00

    Swirl

    Give the dripper one gentle swirl so the grounds settle flat.

  6. 3:00

    Drawdown

    Done when the bed is flat and dry. Pour, wait a minute, then taste as it cools.
