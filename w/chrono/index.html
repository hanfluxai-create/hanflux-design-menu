When will my watch ship?

Series 04 is built between January and March. You get a photo from the bench when assembly starts, and tracking the day it leaves.

Can I pay in parts?

Yes. A third when you reserve, a third when assembly starts and the rest before it ships. No interest and no fees.

Will it fit a small wrist?

The Meridian 39 wears well from 15.5 cm. Below that, the Meridian 36 is the better choice. Both straps are cut in two lengths.

What happens when it needs a service?

We send a prepaid, insured box. The first service is free, later ones are a fixed $240 and take three weeks.
