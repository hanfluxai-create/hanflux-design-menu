How it works

Fifteen minutes a day. Real skills for life.

Short, story-led lessons that kids finish on their own, with Pip cheering every bug they squash.

  1. 1

    Pick a quest

    Choose a world: code, robots, maths or space. Every quest is a story with a goal kids actually care about.

  2. 2

    Play and tinker

    Drag blocks, test, fail, fix. Lessons end at 15 minutes, so screen time stays sensible and nobody has to be the bad guy.

  3. 3

    Make it real

    Every week ends with something to show: a game, an animation, a robot dance. Share it with family in one tap.

FAQ

Questions parents ask us.

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@doodlelabs.app and a real person replies within a day.

What age is Doodle Labs for?

Kids from 6 to 12. Courses adapt as they play, so a 6-year-old and an 11-year-old get very different puzzles inside the same world.

Does my child need to read?

Code Quest is fully voiced and picture-led, so early readers can play solo. The older courses use short, simple text with audio help.

Is it safe for kids?

No ads, no purchases inside the kids' area and no chat with strangers. Your family's data stays private and is never sold.

How does the free week work?

You get full access for seven days. We remind you two days before it ends, and cancelling takes two taps in settings.

Can siblings share one plan?

Yes. Yearly includes three child profiles, so two more kids learn free, each with their own path, progress and creations.

Which devices work?

iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, Chromebooks and any modern browser on a laptop. Progress syncs between them.

Which languages are available?

English and Hindi today, with Tamil and Marathi arriving in early 2027.
