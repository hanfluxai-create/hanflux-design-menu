Our origin story

How the boom began

  1. 2019. A tiny kitchen in Kothrud, one cast-iron griddle and a very heavy spatula.

  2. Friends kept coming back. Then their friends. Then total strangers.

  3. So we bought a tired old truck and painted it the loudest yellow in Pune.

  4. Today: four neighbourhoods, 600 burgers a night. Same griddle.

Vroom!
