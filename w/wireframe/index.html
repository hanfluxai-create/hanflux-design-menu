02Process

How a building gets drawn.

  1. 01

    Sketch

    We walk the site with charcoal and trace until one line feels right: where the light lands, where the wind moves, where the ground wants to be left alone.

    Weeks 1 to 3 · site walks, trace, charcoal

  2. 02

    Model

    That line becomes a white massing model, in card and in code, so you can turn it in your hands and walk it in VR before anything is fixed.

    Weeks 4 to 8 · card models, sun studies

  3. 03

    Detail

    Every junction is drawn at 1:5. Structure, services and finishes live in one coordinated model, so the site never has to guess.

    Months 3 to 6 · about 380 drawings per house

  4. 04

    Build

    We stay on site through handover: weekly visits, a mock-up of every critical junction, and a last walk-through with you at dusk.

    12 to 20 months · site, handover, aftercare

03Approach

We take as little from a site as we can, and give back more light than we block.

  1. Sit lightly

    Pilotis, plinths and cantilevers keep the ground breathing. On the Long House, 70% of the slope was never touched.

  2. Light first

    We model sun paths before floor plans. Every room gets at least two hours of direct light in winter.

  3. Build with what is near

    Laterite in Goa, granite in Coorg, cork and lime in Lisbon. 80% of our materials come from within 200 km of site.

  4. Draw it twice

    Every junction is drawn by two people before it reaches site. Slower on paper, much faster on site.
