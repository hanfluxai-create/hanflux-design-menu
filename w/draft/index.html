M-401What it is made of

The specification, in plain words.

Structure
Reinforced concrete frame with board-formed exposed walls, designed for seismic zone IV and coastal wind loads.
Glazing
Double-glazed, low-iron glass in slim thermally broken aluminium frames, 3.2 metres tall on the sea side.
Stone
Honed pale limestone indoors, the same stone flamed for grip on decks and around the pool.
Timber
Seasoned teak for soffits, ceilings and doors, oiled rather than lacquered so it can be renewed.
Kitchen
Fitted main kitchen with a stone island and integrated appliances, plus a separate working pantry.
Water
Rainwater harvesting to a 60,000 litre tank per villa, softened supply and solar-assisted hot water.
Power backup
Full-load silent generator for the estate, with a 12 kW rooftop solar array on every villa.
Smart home
Lighting, climate, blinds and gate on one wired system, with plain wall switches that always work.
