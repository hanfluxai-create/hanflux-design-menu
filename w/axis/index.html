01 · What we do

Four rings, one plan.

Most families hire an accountant, an estate lawyer and a banker, and hope the three talk. We sit above all of them and keep one written plan that every adviser works from.

01

Tax

We model the next three years of income, gains and distributions before any of it happens. Your accountants still file the returns; we make sure nothing in them is a surprise.

  • Federal, state and city exposure on one page
  • Entity structure and residency reviews
  • Quarterly estimates agreed in advance

02

Ownership and succession

Who owns what, who decides, and what happens on the worst day. We put it in writing while everyone is still on speaking terms.

  • Trusts, wills and operating agreements reconciled
  • Buy-sell terms and a valuation method
  • A plan for the first 30 days after a founder steps back

03

Family governance

A business survives a bad quarter more easily than a bad Thanksgiving. We run the conversations most families put off, with an agenda and a neutral chair.

  • Family meetings, twice a year, minuted
  • Roles and pay for relatives in the business
  • Owner education for the next generation

04

Deals

A sale, a raise or a buyout. We prepare you 12 to 18 months ahead, then sit on your side of the table through close. We take no success fee, so our advice does not move with the price.

  • Pre-sale structuring and tax modelling
  • Term sheet and earn-out review
  • A liquidity plan for the day after

02 · The first 90 days

Thirteen weeks from first call to a plan in motion.

Every engagement opens the same way. You know what arrives, and in which week, before you sign anything.

  1. Weeks 1 to 2

    Listen and collect

    Two working sessions with you, then we gather every return, trust, agreement and cap table from your existing advisers.

    You receive
    A document inventory and a list of what is missing

  2. Weeks 3 to 5

    The map

    We draw the whole picture: entities, owners, tax positions, family roles and the promises nobody wrote down.

    You receive
    One page showing everything you own and who controls it

  3. Weeks 6 to 9

    The plan

    We rank what to fix, what each fix saves or protects, and who has to sign. Then we walk the family through it.

    You receive
    A written plan of 10 to 15 ranked actions with costs and dates

  4. Weeks 10 to 13

    In motion

    We brief your lawyers and accountants, start the first actions and put the year on a calendar.

    You receive
    A 12-month calendar and a standing quarterly review
