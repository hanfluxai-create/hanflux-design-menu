One platform, three worlds

Follow the box from dock to doorstep.

  1. From dock door to departure.

    Every pallet is scanned at the dock door, so a shipment is live the moment it is packed. Cargoflow reads your WMS events and carrier pickups and stitches them into one timeline.

    • Dock scans in real time
    • Pickup windows from 120+ carriers
    • Auto-booked carrier slots

    4 min from packing to first tracking event

  2. Ocean freight without blind spots.

    Vessel positions, berth schedules and customs holds in one view. When a ship slips its ETA, Cargoflow recalculates every order on board and flags the ones that will miss their promise date.

    • Live vessel positions
    • Customs hold alerts
    • Container-level ETAs

    2.6 days earlier warning on ocean delays

  3. Live all the way to the doorstep.

    Vans, couriers and drone drops report back to one map. Customers get a branded tracking page and a heads-up before the doorbell rings, not after it doesn’t.

    • Branded tracking pages
    • Proactive SMS and email
    • Photo proof of delivery

    41% fewer “where is my order” tickets
