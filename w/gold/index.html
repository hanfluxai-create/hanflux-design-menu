Since 1962

One workbench in Johari Bazaar. Three generations later.

In 1962 Harilal Soni rented a single bench off Johari Bazaar and set stones for the jewellers on the street. His son Mohan opened our first showroom in 1988. In 2009 his granddaughter Varsha took the house to Mumbai and gave it the name on the door today.

The bench is still in the atelier, and so is the rule written above it: a piece leaves when it is ready, never when it is due.
