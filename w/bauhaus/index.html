Courses

Four courses. One way of seeing.

Every course runs for eight weeks with two live sessions a week, a studio brief every Friday and written feedback on everything you make.

018 weeks

Typography

Letters, grids and the rules worth breaking. Set type for print and screen, then draw a display face of your own.

  • You will make A 24-page type specimen
  • Live Tue and Thu, 18:00 GMT

$390

028 weeks

Colour Theory

Hue, value and temperature, learned by painting before picking. Build palettes that hold up on paper and in code.

  • You will make A 12-swatch colour system
  • Live Mon and Wed, 17:00 GMT

$390

0310 weeks

Product Design

From sketch to working prototype: research, form, materials and the honest testing in between.

  • You will make A desk object, prototyped
  • Live Tue and Sat, 15:00 GMT

$390

048 weeks

Motion Basics

Timing, easing and rhythm. Make shapes move with weight, then make interfaces feel alive.

  • You will make A 30-second title sequence
  • Live Wed and Fri, 18:30 GMT

$390

Learn by making

Make a poster a day. Keep the good ones.

Every student starts the morning with a fifteen-minute composition study: one grid, five shapes, three colours. The wall below draws new ones as you watch.

1,284 studies made this cohort
