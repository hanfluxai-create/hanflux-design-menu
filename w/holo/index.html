07FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Still curious? Write to hello@prismcard.co and a human replies within a day.

When does Prism ship?

We invite the waitlist in batches from spring 2027. Your place in line decides your batch, and every friend who joins with your link moves you up 500 spots.

Is there a credit check to join the waitlist?

No. Joining the waitlist is free and has no effect on your credit score. A check only happens if you accept an invitation and choose to apply.

How does 3% back actually work?

You earn 3% on every purchase up to $3,000 of spend a month and 1.5% after that. Cashback lands in your balance daily, in dollars, with nothing to activate.

Is there really no annual fee?

Really. No annual fee, no foreign transaction fees and no charge for virtual cards. If we ever launch a paid tier, the card you hold stays free.

What is the card made of?

An 18 g core of laser-cut 316L stainless steel, a 12-micron holographic film on the front and a laser-etched back. Send it back when it expires and we recycle the steel.

Can I use Prism abroad?

Yes, anywhere contactless cards are accepted, with 0% foreign transaction fees and live exchange rates shown in the app.
