FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Write to hello@ledgerly.app and a human replies within a working day.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Up to three clients and unlimited invoices, for as long as you like. You only pay standard processing fees when a client pays by card or bank transfer.

How do payment links work?

Every invoice gets a secure link. Your client opens it, picks card or bank transfer and pays in two taps. Funds reach your account in about two business days.

Can clients pay in their own currency?

Yes. Invoice in 135 currencies. We convert at the live rate on the day of payment and record everything in your home currency for tax time.

Will my accountant like it?

Invite them for free. They get read-only access, clean CSV and PDF exports, and a quarterly report that matches your bank feed line for line.

What happens when a client pays late?

Ledgerly sends up to three reminders on your schedule and in your tone, and can add a late fee if you switch it on. Reminders stop the moment the invoice is paid.

Can I cancel anytime?

Anytime, in two clicks. Your invoices and reports stay downloadable and your account drops back to the Free plan.
