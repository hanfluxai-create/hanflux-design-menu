02Method

How we work is written down before we start.

A

A partner on every call

The partner who scopes your matter runs it. There are no hand-offs after the first meeting.

B

Scope and fee, in writing

You see the scope, the team and the fee before any work begins. Changes are agreed, never discovered on an invoice.

C

Advice in plain English

Every memo opens with the answer, the risk and the decision you need to make, in that order.

D

Two working days

Every new query receives a considered first response within two working days, from the partner on it.
