02The leak

Every unanswered ring is a buyer on someone else’s lot.

You already paid for that lead: the ads, the listings, the site. Then it called, and the phone did what phones do at 7 PM on a Tuesday.

  • Slow response

    The buyer who called at noon gets a callback at four. By then they test-drove the same car across town.

  • Missed calls

    Saturday rush, one BDC rep, six lines ringing. Somebody always loses, and it’s usually the store.

  • Inconsistent follow-up

    Follow-up depends on who’s on shift and how their day is going. Your pipeline shouldn’t.

  • After-hours gaps

    Buyers shop at 9 PM. Dealerships answer at 9 AM. Twelve hours is a long time to hold a purchase decision.

  • Fragmented systems

    Phone, CRM, calendar and inventory each tell a different story about the same customer. Nobody reconciles them at 8 PM.
