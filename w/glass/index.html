FAQ

Questions, answered calmly.

Still unsure? Message us on WhatsApp and a dentist replies, not a bot.

Will it hurt?

We use computer-controlled anaesthesia that numbs slowly, so most patients feel only gentle pressure. Raise a hand at any point and we pause, no questions asked.

Are the prices on this page final?

Yes. Your written plan lists the exact price before we start. If anything changes mid-treatment, we explain and ask first. You never pay for something you did not approve.

How late are you open?

Till 9 PM Monday to Saturday and till 7 PM on Sundays. Evening slots fill first, so booking two or three days ahead is a good idea.

Do you accept insurance?

We give itemised invoices and reports that most health insurers accept for reimbursement, and cashless billing with select corporate plans. Share your policy on WhatsApp and we will check it for you.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Send us a photo on WhatsApp. We keep two emergency slots open every evening for pain, swelling or a broken tooth.

Is there parking nearby?

Six free car spaces in our basement and a two-wheeler bay. If you come by metro, we are a five-minute walk from Indiranagar station.
