Questions, answered.

Still curious? Message us on WhatsApp. A real person replies, usually with a snack in hand.

Are they really not fried?

Really. Our protein chips are baked in small batches and our makhana is slow-roasted in drum roasters. Seasoning goes on with a light mist of rice bran oil. Nothing ever touches a fryer.

Why no palm oil?

Because it is cheap, not good. We use rice bran oil and print exactly how much is in every pack, down to the gram.

How much protein is in a pack?

Protein chips carry 12 g in a 60 g pack. Roasted makhana carries 6 to 7 g in a 70 g pack, which beats most of what your hand finds at 5 pm.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes, to more than 19,000 pin codes. Delivery is free above ₹499 and most orders arrive in 2 to 5 days.

Are they gluten free?

The makhana range is naturally gluten free. Protein chips are made in a kitchen that also handles wheat, so we do not label them gluten free.

Can I swap flavours after I order a box?

Yes, until it is packed. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and we will swap it, no forms.
