Before you book

Questions owners ask.

Who is this for?

Businesses that already spend money or time making the phone ring: insurance agencies, dealerships, home services, real estate teams, law firms and service businesses. If leads and calls drive your revenue, it's for you. If you get a handful of calls a month, it isn't yet.

Will it sound like a robot?

It's tuned to your brand and built to sound natural. You hear it live on your audit before you decide anything. It also tells callers they're speaking with an AI assistant. That disclosure is built in, not optional.

We already have a receptionist or a BDC. Why add this?

Oraya covers what your people can't: after-hours, overflow, lunch breaks, instant lead follow-up and reminders. Hot or complex calls go to your team live, with context. Your staff spends their time on conversations that close.

Will it work with our phone system and CRM?

It runs on the numbers, calendar and CRM you already use. We confirm your exact setup on the audit before anything is installed.

What happens on the audit call?

30 minutes on a screen share. Bring a recent call or lead report if you have one. We run the leak math on your numbers, show you where revenue is slipping, and run Oraya live. No slides, no pressure.

How fast can we go live?

We target 72 hours from kickoff to your first live line. We start with the leak that costs you the most, then add channels once it's working.

Is it compliant?

Compliance is built into how it runs: consent checked before every outbound call or text, recording disclosure, AI identification, Do Not Call checks and a consent record for every contact. TCPA damages start at $500 per violation and can reach $1,500 if willful, so we treat compliance as a hard floor, not a feature.

Who owns the data?

You do. Oraya works as your service provider. If you leave, we purge your data.

How much is it?

Monthly, priced by the channels and volume you need. You'll see the exact number on the audit, built from your own call and lead data, before you commit to anything.
