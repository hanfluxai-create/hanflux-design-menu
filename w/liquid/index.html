The atelier

Distilled in Jaipur. Finished in Paris.

Our materials begin in a courtyard workshop in Jaipur, where saffron, rose and vetiver are distilled in copper deg and bhapka stills, the way attars have been made for four hundred years.

They travel to our atelier in the Marais, where each extrait rests, is cold-filtered twice and bottled by hand. Every bottle carries its batch number and the initials of the person who poured it.
