03 · Process

Five stages. No surprises.

  1. Weeks 1 to 2

    Survey

    Total station and drone survey, soil tests to 12 m and a utilities check before a single line is drawn.

  2. Weeks 3 to 10

    Design

    Architecture, structure and services coordinated in one model. You sign off a fixed scope and programme.

  3. Weeks 6 to 16

    Approvals

    We file and follow up building permission, fire NOC and environment clearance with the local authorities.

  4. Months 4 to 22

    Build

    Weekly photo reports, a live programme on your phone and one site engineer who answers the call.

  5. Month 23

    Handover

    Snags closed, as-built drawings, O&M manuals and a five year structural warranty.
